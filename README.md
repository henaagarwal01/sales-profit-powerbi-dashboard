# 📊 Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to analyze sales performance, profitability, and the impact of discount strategies. It enables data-driven decision-making by uncovering trends, patterns, and key business insights.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
Businesses often struggle to balance **sales growth and profitability**, especially due to heavy discounting and varying regional performance. This dashboard helps identify the key factors affecting profit and supports smarter business strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Data Visualization  
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data Source  
- **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- **DAX** – Calculated Measures  

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Removed missing and duplicate values  
- Standardized column names and formats  
- Converted date fields for time-based analysis  
- Created calculated columns and measures using DAX  

---

## 📁 Dataset Description
The dataset includes:
- Sales Amount  
- Profit  
- Discount  
- Product Category & Sub-category  
- Customer Segment  
- Region  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Overview
- Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Average Discount  

### 🔹 Profit by Sub-Category
- Identifies top and low-performing products  

### 🔹 Regional Sales Analysis
- Compares performance across regions  

### 🔹 Sales Trend Over Time
- Monthly trends and seasonal patterns  

### 🔹 Discount vs Profit Analysis
- Shows impact of discounting on profitability  

### 🔹 Interactive Filters
- Enables segment-wise and region-wise exploration  

---

## 🔥 Key Insights
- 📉 High discounts (above ~20%) reduce profit margins significantly  
- 💻 Technology category generates the highest profit  
- 🌍 West region leads in sales, but profit varies by category  
- 📈 Strong sales growth observed in Q4 (Oct–Dec)  
- ⚠️ Some sub-categories show consistent low or negative profit  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products  
- Focus on expanding high-profit categories like Technology  
- Improve or reprice underperforming sub-categories  
- Target high-performing regions with marketing strategies  

---

## 📐 Sample DAX Measures
- Total Profit = SUM(Profit)  
- Profit Margin = DIVIDE(Profit, Sales, 0)
